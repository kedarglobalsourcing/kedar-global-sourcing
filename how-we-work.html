Our Process

Eight steps designed around the buyer's requirement

We begin with the requirement rather than a fixed catalogue. Each stage is coordinated against the product, specification, quantity, packaging, destination and commercial needs of the buyer.

01

Buyer Requirement

We clarify the product, specification, quantity, packaging, destination and required timeline.

02

Supplier Identification

We work to identify suitable Indian suppliers that can match the requested product and commercial parameters.

03

Supplier & Product Evaluation

Relevant supplier capability, product information and available documentation are reviewed against the requirement.

04

Quotation & Sample Coordination

Quotations, specifications and samples can be coordinated where required before commercial commitment.

05

Commercial Alignment

Pricing, quantity, packaging, payment and other order terms are aligned before proceeding.

06

Documentation & Export Readiness

Required product, compliance and export documentation is coordinated with the selected supplier.

07

Shipment Coordination

Packaging, dispatch readiness and shipment-related requirements are coordinated against agreed terms.

08

Post-Shipment Coordination

We remain available for relevant communication and follow-up connected with the completed order.

What We Need From You

A useful enquiry starts with clear information

The more detail you provide at the beginning, the more effectively suitable supply options can be assessed.

ProductProduct name, form, grade or intended application.
SpecificationRequired quality parameters, size, mesh, composition or other technical details.
QuantityApproximate order quantity and, where relevant, expected purchase frequency.
PackagingBulk, retail-ready or buyer-specific packaging and labelling requirements.
DestinationDestination country and preferably the required port or delivery location.
Documents & TimelineRequired certificates, reports or declarations and your target procurement timeline.
Our Sourcing Approach

Requirement first. Supplier selection second.

Our role is to help narrow the gap between an overseas buyer's requirement and suitable sourcing possibilities in India. We avoid treating supplier-specific capabilities as universal claims.

Requirement-led

Supplier options are considered against the buyer's actual product and commercial requirement.

Transparent communication

Specifications, availability, documents and commercial points are clarified rather than assumed.

Supplier-specific verification

Capabilities and available documentation are checked with the selected supplier for the relevant enquiry.

Supplier Selection

Finding a suitable supply option, not simply a supplier name

We consider the buyer's specification, quantity, packaging, destination and commercial requirements when identifying potential supply options. The objective is to find a practical fit for the enquiry rather than push a one-size-fits-all catalogue.

Quality & documentation

Product specifications, samples, test reports, certificates, declarations and export documentation can be coordinated according to the buyer's requirement and the selected supplier's capability.

Important: Required documents or certifications should be stated during the enquiry so their availability can be verified before an order is finalized.
