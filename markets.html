Destination Requirements

Every market can have different buyer and compliance needs

International sourcing is not only about finding a product. The destination market can influence packaging, labelling, documentation, testing and other commercial requirements. We therefore ask buyers to specify these needs at enquiry stage wherever possible.

Product Specification Grade, form, particle size, mesh, quality parameters or other technical requirements.
Packaging & Labelling Bulk packs, buyer-specific packs, labels or markings subject to supplier capability.
Testing & Documentation Test reports, declarations, certificates or documents requested by the buyer.
Destination & Port Country and port information helps align logistics and order documentation.
Commercial Terms Quantity, Incoterms, payment expectations and shipment timing can affect supplier selection.
Market-specific Requirements Any destination-specific conditions should be stated so they can be checked before commitment.
Our Approach

Destination-led sourcing, not one-size-fits-all exporting

We begin with the buyer's requirement and destination, then work to identify suitable Indian supply options. This helps avoid assuming that one supplier, specification or documentation set will suit every market.

What We Coordinate
Supplier Fit Product and commercial suitability for the buyer requirement.
Documentation Fit Availability of required supplier and product documents.
Packaging Fit Buyer and destination packaging requirements where feasible.
Shipment Readiness Coordination of agreed requirements before dispatch.
