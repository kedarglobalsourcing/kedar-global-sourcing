About Kedar Global Sourcing

Your sourcing bridge to India

Kedar Global Sourcing is an India-based sourcing and trading business focused on connecting international buyers with suitable Indian suppliers. We work from the buyer's actual requirement and coordinate sourcing, commercial communication, documentation and shipment readiness.

Who We Are

Built around clarity, responsiveness and execution

Kedar Global Sourcing was established to help overseas buyers access Indian sourcing opportunities through a clear and requirement-led process. Rather than pushing a fixed catalogue, we begin by understanding the product, specification, quantity, packaging and destination required by the buyer.

Based on these requirements, we work to identify suitable supply options, coordinate communication with suppliers and help align commercial and export-related requirements before shipment.

Our operating principle

Good sourcing starts with understanding the buyer's requirement. We focus on clear specifications, transparent communication and suitable supplier selection rather than a one-size-fits-all approach.

Our Approach

Requirement-led sourcing

Every buyer may have different specifications, quantities, packaging, documentation and destination requirements. Our model is designed around those differences.

Our Commitment
