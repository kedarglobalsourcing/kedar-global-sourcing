About Kedar

A sourcing partner built around clarity and execution

Kedar Global Sourcing is a newly established proprietorship in India. The business is being developed to connect overseas buyers with suitable Indian suppliers while coordinating requirements, samples, commercial communication and export readiness.

Our operating principle

We prefer clear specifications, transparent communication and supplier selection based on the buyer's actual requirement rather than pushing a fixed catalogue.
