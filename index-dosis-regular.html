About Kedar

Your sourcing bridge to India

Kedar Global Sourcing is an India-based sourcing and international trade business focused on connecting overseas buyers with suitable Indian manufacturers and suppliers.

We work from the buyer's requirement — including specification, quantity, packaging and destination — and coordinate supplier identification, commercial communication, samples and export readiness.

Our operating principle

Good sourcing starts with understanding the buyer's requirement. We focus on clear specifications, transparent communication and suitable supplier selection rather than a one-size-fits-all catalogue.

Our commitment
