How We Source

Buyer requirement first, supplier selection second

We avoid presenting supplier-specific claims as universal product specifications. Instead, we match the buyer's requirement with suitable supply options and verify commercial and documentation details before order confirmation.

Requirement Review

Product, specification, quantity, packaging and destination are clarified first.

Supplier Comparison

Suitable Indian suppliers are evaluated against the requested parameters.

Document Verification

Available product and compliance documents are checked with the selected supplier.

Sample Coordination

Samples can be coordinated where required before commercial commitment.

Commercial Alignment

Pricing, quantity, packaging and order terms are aligned before proceeding.

Shipment Readiness

Packaging, documentation and dispatch readiness are coordinated for the agreed order.

Packaging & Commercial Requirements

Built around the buyer's order requirement

Packaging, labelling, quantity, shipment destination and commercial requirements can be discussed as part of the sourcing process. Availability depends on the selected supplier and confirmed order terms.

Quality & documentation

Required product documentation, test reports, certificates or compliance documents should be specified by the buyer at enquiry stage. We then check availability with the selected supplier rather than assuming that every supplier carries the same documentation.
